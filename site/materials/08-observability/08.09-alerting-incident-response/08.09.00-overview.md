# Chapter 8.9: Alerting and Incident Response

## Overview
This chapter covers designing effective alerting strategies that prevent alert fatigue while ensuring critical issues are caught. We'll explore symptom vs cause-based alerting, alert severity classification, runbook creation, and on-call best practices to build reliable incident response workflows.

## Key Concepts
- **Alert Design Principles:** creating actionable, business-impact focused alerts.
- **Symptom vs Cause-based Alerting:** alerting on user impact rather than infrastructure failures.
- **Alert Severity and Routing:** classifying alerts and directing them to appropriate responders.
- **Runbooks and Playbooks:** documenting procedures for consistent incident response.
- **On-call Practices:** building sustainable on-call rotations and incident management workflows.
