# Chapter 8.1: Observability Fundamentals

## Overview
This chapter introduces observability: what it is, why it matters, and how it differs from traditional monitoring. You'll understand the three pillars—logs, metrics, and traces—and learn how they work together to provide insights into system behavior. This foundation is essential for operating reliable distributed systems.

## Key Concepts
- **Monitoring vs Observability:** understanding the difference and when to use each approach.
- **The Three Pillars:** logs, metrics, and traces as complementary telemetry signals.
- **Observability-Driven Development:** building systems with observability in mind from the start.
- **Cardinality and Dimensionality:** managing high-cardinality data and metric dimensions.
- **Correlation:** connecting logs, metrics, and traces for comprehensive debugging.
