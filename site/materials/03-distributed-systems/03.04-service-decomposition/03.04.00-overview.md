# Chapter 3.4: Service Decomposition Strategies

## Overview
This chapter explores the critical challenge of decomposing monolithic applications into microservices. You'll learn proven strategies for identifying service boundaries, including decomposition by business capability, using Domain-Driven Design principles, and applying the Strangler Fig pattern for incremental migration. We'll also tackle the difficult problem of database decomposition and how to avoid creating distributed monoliths.

## Key Concepts
- **Business Capability Mapping:** identifying services based on what the business does.
- **DDD Bounded Contexts:** using Domain-Driven Design to define clear service boundaries.
- **Strangler Fig Pattern:** incrementally migrating from monolith to microservices.
- **Database Decomposition:** splitting monolithic databases while maintaining data integrity.
- **Avoiding Pitfalls:** recognizing and avoiding distributed monolith anti-patterns.
