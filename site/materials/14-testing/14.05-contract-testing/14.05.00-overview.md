# Chapter 14.5: Contract Testing

## Overview
Implement consumer-driven contract testing with Pact to validate service integrations without expensive end-to-end tests. Learn how to write consumer contracts, verify provider implementations, and integrate contract testing into your CI/CD pipeline.

## Key Concepts
- **Consumer-Driven Contracts:** consumers define expectations about provider APIs.
- **Pact Framework:** the de-facto standard for contract testing in .NET.
- **Provider Verification:** replaying consumer contracts against provider implementations.
- **Contract Versioning:** managing contract evolution and breaking changes.
- **Pact Broker:** centralizing contract storage and verification results.
