# Chapter 14.1: Testing Philosophy and Strategy

## Overview
This chapter explores the philosophy behind effective testing strategies. We'll examine the testing pyramid, understand test economics (cost vs value), explore risk-based testing approaches, learn about testing quadrants, and develop comprehensive test strategies. The goal is to make informed decisions about where to invest testing effort for maximum confidence and value.

## Key Concepts
- **Testing Pyramid:** a framework for organizing tests by granularity and quantity.
- **Test Economics:** understanding the cost-benefit relationship of different test types.
- **Risk-Based Testing:** prioritizing testing efforts based on risk assessment.
- **Testing Quadrants:** a model for categorizing and planning different types of tests.
- **Test Strategy:** a comprehensive plan for testing approach and execution.
