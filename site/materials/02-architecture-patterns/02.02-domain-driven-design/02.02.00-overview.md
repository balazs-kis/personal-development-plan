# Chapter 2.2: Domain-Driven Design Fundamentals

## Overview
Learn the strategic and tactical patterns of DDD, including ubiquitous language, bounded contexts, aggregates, entities, value objects, domain events, and repositories. DDD provides a framework for modeling complex business domains and aligning software design with business needs.

## Key Concepts
- **Ubiquitous Language:** creating a shared language between developers and domain experts.
- **Bounded Contexts:** defining clear boundaries for different parts of the domain.
- **Aggregates:** designing consistency boundaries and aggregate roots.
- **Entities vs Value Objects:** understanding identity and immutability.
- **Domain Events:** modeling business events explicitly in the domain.
