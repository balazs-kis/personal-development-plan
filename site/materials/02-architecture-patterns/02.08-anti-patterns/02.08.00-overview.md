# Chapter 2.8: Anti-Patterns and Technical Debt

## Overview
Learn to recognize common architectural anti-patterns (Big Ball of Mud, Golden Hammer, Cargo Cult), understand technical debt as a concept, and strategies for managing and paying it down. Knowing what not to do is as important as knowing what to do—recognizing anti-patterns early prevents them from becoming entrenched.

## Key Concepts
- **Common Anti-Patterns:** recognizing Big Ball of Mud, Golden Hammer, and more.
- **Technical Debt:** understanding debt as intentional and unintentional.
- **Debt Management:** tracking and prioritizing technical debt.
- **Refactoring Strategies:** systematic approaches to improving code.
- **Pragmatic Decisions:** knowing when to live with debt vs pay it down.
