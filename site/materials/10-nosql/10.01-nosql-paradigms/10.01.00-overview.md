# Chapter 10.1: NoSQL Paradigms

## Overview
This chapter surveys the NoSQL landscape: document stores, key-value stores, column-family stores, and graph databases. You'll understand the CAP theorem implications and when each type is appropriate.

## Key Concepts
- **Document Databases:** MongoDB, CouchDB, and flexible schema design.
- **Key-Value Stores:** Redis, etcd for high-speed simple lookups.
- **Column-Family Stores:** Cassandra, HBase for large-scale data storage.
- **Graph Databases:** Neo4j, JanusGraph for relationship-heavy data.
- **CAP Theorem:** understanding consistency, availability, and partition tolerance trade-offs.
