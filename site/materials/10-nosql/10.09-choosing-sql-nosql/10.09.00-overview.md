# Chapter 10.9: Choosing Between SQL and NoSQL

## Overview
Develop a framework for choosing between SQL and NoSQL: evaluating requirements, understanding trade-offs, and recognizing when polyglot persistence is appropriate.

## Key Concepts
- **Data model considerations:** Structured vs unstructured data requirements.
- **Consistency requirements:** ACID vs eventual consistency trade-offs.
- **Scalability needs:** Vertical vs horizontal scaling approaches.
- **Query patterns:** OLTP vs OLAP workload characteristics.
- **Team expertise and operational concerns:** Skills, tooling, and maintenance.
