---

## Introduction

Ranking functions are specialized window functions that assign ranks or positions to rows within a partition. They're essential for top-N queries, percentile calculations, pagination, and any scenario where you need to order and rank data.

## Key Concepts Summary

### ROW_NUMBER(): Unique Sequential Numbers

Assigns a unique number to each row, starting from 1:

```sql
-- Basic row numbering
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as row_num
FROM products;

-- With PARTITION BY: restart numbering for each group
SELECT 
    category,
    product_name,
    price,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
        PARTITION BY category 
        ORDER BY price DESC
    ) as rank_in_category
FROM products;

-- Use case: pagination
SELECT *
FROM (
    SELECT 
        *,
        ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY created_at DESC) as row_num
    FROM articles
) sub
WHERE row_num BETWEEN 11 AND 20;  -- Page 2, 10 items per page
```

### RANK(): Ranking with Gaps

Assigns ranks with gaps when there are ties:

```sql
-- If two products tie for 2nd place, next rank is 4
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as rank
FROM products;

-- Result example:
-- Rank | Product   | Price
--    1 | Product A | 100
--    2 | Product B | 90
--    2 | Product C | 90   -- Tie
--    4 | Product D | 85   -- Gap (skipped 3)
--    5 | Product E | 80
```

### DENSE_RANK(): Ranking Without Gaps

Assigns ranks without gaps when there are ties:

```sql
-- No gaps in ranking sequence
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as dense_rank
FROM products;

-- Result example:
-- Rank | Product   | Price
--    1 | Product A | 100
--    2 | Product B | 90
--    2 | Product C | 90   -- Tie
--    3 | Product D | 85   -- No gap
--    4 | Product E | 80
```

### Comparing the Three Ranking Functions

```sql
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as row_num,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as rank,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY price DESC) as dense_rank
FROM products;

-- Example output:
-- Product   | Price | row_num | rank | dense_rank
-- A         |   100 |       1 |    1 |          1
-- B         |    90 |       2 |    2 |          2
-- C         |    90 |       3 |    2 |          2  -- Tie
-- D         |    85 |       4 |    4 |          3  -- Note differences
-- E         |    80 |       5 |    5 |          4
```

### NTILE(): Dividing into Buckets

Distributes rows into a specified number of groups:

```sql
-- Divide products into 4 quartiles by price
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    NTILE(4) OVER (ORDER BY price) as price_quartile
FROM products;

-- Use case: top/middle/bottom performers
SELECT 
    employee_name,
    sales_total,
    NTILE(3) OVER (ORDER BY sales_total DESC) as performance_tier
FROM employee_sales;
-- Tier 1: top third
-- Tier 2: middle third
-- Tier 3: bottom third
```

### Finding Top N Per Group

A common pattern using ROW_NUMBER or RANK:

```sql
-- Top 3 products by revenue in each category
SELECT *
FROM (
    SELECT 
        category,
        product_name,
        revenue,
        ROW_NUMBER() OVER (
            PARTITION BY category 
            ORDER BY revenue DESC
        ) as rank_in_category
    FROM products
) ranked
WHERE rank_in_category <= 3;
```

### Percentile Ranking

```sql
-- PERCENT_RANK: relative rank (0 to 1)
SELECT 
    student_name,
    score,
    PERCENT_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY score DESC) as percentile
FROM exam_scores;

-- CUME_DIST: cumulative distribution (percentage of values <= current)
SELECT 
    product_name,
    price,
    CUME_DIST() OVER (ORDER BY price) as cumulative_dist
FROM products;
```

### Practical Example: Sales Leaderboard

```sql
-- Complete sales leaderboard with multiple rankings
SELECT 
    salesperson_name,
    total_sales,
    ROW_NUMBER() OVER (ORDER BY total_sales DESC) as position,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY total_sales DESC) as rank_with_ties,
    DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY total_sales DESC) as dense_rank,
    NTILE(10) OVER (ORDER BY total_sales DESC) as decile,
    ROUND(PERCENT_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY total_sales DESC) * 100, 2) as percentile
FROM sales_summary
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;
```
