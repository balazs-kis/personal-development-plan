# Chapter 15.8: Managing Technical Debt

## Overview

Strategically manage technical debt: identifying and categorizing debt, communicating debt to stakeholders, prioritizing payoff, and preventing accumulation. Technical debt is inevitable, but unmanaged debt slows development and increases risk. Strategic management balances short-term delivery with long-term health.

## Key Concepts

- **Technical Debt Types and Sources:** Understanding different categories of debt and how they accumulate.
- **Debt Inventory and Tracking:** Building systematic approaches to identify and measure technical debt.
- **Communicating Debt to Non-Technical Stakeholders:** Translating technical concerns into business impact.
- **Prioritization Frameworks:** Deciding which debt to pay down first based on impact and cost.
- **Prevention Strategies:** Building practices that prevent debt accumulation from the start.
