# Chapter 15.4: Architectural Decision Records (ADRs)

## Overview
Document architectural decisions using ADRs: structure, writing effective ADRs, building decision logs, and using ADRs for communication.

## Key Concepts
- **ADR Structure:** capturing context, decision, and consequences.
- **Effective Writing:** focusing on the "why" and trade-offs.
- **Decision Lifecycle:** proposed, accepted, superseded, deprecated states.
- **Decision Logs:** building and maintaining an ADR repository.
- **Communication Tool:** using ADRs for team alignment and knowledge transfer.
