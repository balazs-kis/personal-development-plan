# Chapter 15.1: The Staff+ Engineer Role

## Overview

This chapter explores what staff, principal, and architect roles entail: scope of influence, expectations, and how these roles differ from senior engineers. Understanding the staff+ role is crucial for anyone looking to advance beyond senior engineering while remaining on the technical track.

## Key Concepts

- **Staff+ Role Archetypes:** Tech Lead, Architect, Solver, and Right Hand—each with distinct responsibilities and organizational fit.
- **Scope and Influence:** Shifting from team-level execution to organizational-level impact.
- **Technical vs People Leadership:** Leading through influence, expertise, and strategy rather than direct reports.
- **Measuring Impact at Scale:** Moving from individual contributions to multiplier effects across teams.
- **The "Glue Work" of Senior Technical Roles:** Essential but often invisible work that holds teams and projects together.
