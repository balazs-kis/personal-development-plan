# Chapter 15.2: System Design Fundamentals

## Overview

This chapter teaches you to approach system design problems systematically: gathering requirements, estimating capacity, identifying components, analyzing trade-offs, and refining your design iteratively. These skills are essential for staff+ engineers and are the foundation of system design interviews.

## Key Concepts

- **Requirements Gathering:** Distinguishing functional from non-functional requirements and asking the right questions.
- **Capacity Estimation:** Back-of-envelope calculations to validate design feasibility.
- **Component Identification:** Breaking systems into logical building blocks.
- **Trade-off Analysis:** Understanding and communicating the pros and cons of design decisions.
- **Iterative Refinement:** Improving designs through structured feedback and testing.
