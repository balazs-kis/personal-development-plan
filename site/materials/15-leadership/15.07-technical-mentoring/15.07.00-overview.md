# Chapter 15.7: Technical Mentoring

## Overview

Mentor other engineers effectively: structuring mentorship, giving actionable feedback, supporting growth, and creating learning opportunities. Staff+ engineers are expected to multiply their impact through others. Effective mentoring develops team capability and is deeply rewarding.

## Key Concepts

- **Mentoring vs Coaching vs Sponsoring:** Understanding the distinct roles and when to use each approach.
- **Structuring Mentorship Relationships:** Designing time-bound, goal-oriented mentorship programs.
- **Giving Actionable Feedback:** Providing specific, constructive feedback that drives improvement.
- **Creating Growth Opportunities:** Identifying and designing stretch assignments and learning opportunities.
- **Measuring Mentoring Impact:** Evaluating mentorship effectiveness and outcomes.
