# Chapter 15.9: Leading Without Authority

## Overview
This chapter explores how to influence technical direction and drive change without formal authority—a critical skill for Staff+ engineers and senior individual contributors. You'll learn how to build credibility, create alignment across teams, navigate disagreement constructively, drive incremental change, and build coalitions to multiply your impact.

## Key Concepts
- **Building Technical Credibility:** establishing trust and influence through expertise and consistent delivery.
- **Creating Alignment:** getting stakeholders and teams moving in the same direction without direct control.
- **Navigating Disagreement:** handling technical conflicts and building consensus productively.
- **Driving Change Incrementally:** making meaningful progress through small, strategic steps.
- **Building Coalitions:** creating networks of support to amplify your influence and impact.
