# Chapter 15.3: Distributed System Design Patterns

## Overview
Master patterns used in system design: load balancing, caching layers, database scaling, message queues, and CDNs. Understand when and why to apply each.

## Key Concepts
- **Load Balancing:** distributing traffic across servers for reliability and scalability.
- **Caching Layers:** CDN, application, and database-level caching strategies.
- **Database Scaling:** replication, partitioning, and sharding.
- **Message Queues:** decoupling services with asynchronous messaging.
- **Rate Limiting:** protecting systems from overload and abuse.
