# Chapter 13.4: GitLab CI/CD

## Overview
Learn to implement CI/CD with GitLab, a comprehensive DevOps platform with integrated CI/CD capabilities. We'll explore `.gitlab-ci.yml` syntax, pipeline stages, artifacts and caching, environments, and GitLab-specific features including Auto DevOps and runners.

## Key Concepts
- **.gitlab-ci.yml Configuration:** YAML-based pipeline definitions with jobs and stages.
- **Stages & Dependencies:** organizing work into sequential stages or DAG-based execution.
- **Artifacts & Caching:** passing build outputs between jobs and optimizing performance.
- **Environments:** deployment targets with protection rules and approval gates.
- **GitLab Runners:** execution environments with Docker, shell, and Kubernetes executors.
