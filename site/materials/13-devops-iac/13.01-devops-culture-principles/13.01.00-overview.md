# Chapter 13.1: DevOps Culture and Principles

## Overview
This chapter explores DevOps as a cultural shift and philosophy rather than just a set of tools. We'll examine the principles that underpin successful DevOps transformations, focusing on breaking down silos, fostering shared responsibility, and creating a culture of continuous improvement. You'll learn the CALMS framework (Culture, Automation, Lean, Measurement, Sharing) and understand how to advocate for and implement DevOps practices effectively in your organization.

## Key Concepts
- **DevOps Cultural Principles:** Understanding DevOps as a philosophy centered on collaboration, transparency, and shared goals.
- **CALMS Framework:** Culture, Automation, Lean, Measurement, and Sharing as foundational pillars of DevOps.
- **Breaking Down Silos:** Eliminating barriers between development, operations, and business teams.
- **Shared Responsibility:** Building accountability across teams for software delivery and operations.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Creating feedback loops and fostering a learning culture.
