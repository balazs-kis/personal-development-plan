# Chapter 15.10: System Design Interview Preparation

## Overview
This chapter prepares you for system design interviews—a critical component of senior+ engineering interviews at top companies. You'll learn the interview format and expectations, master common question patterns, develop effective communication techniques, practice handling ambiguity, and build a comprehensive preparation strategy. These interviews test your ability to design scalable systems while thinking aloud and navigating trade-offs under pressure.

## Key Concepts
- **Interview Structure and Expectations:** understanding the format, evaluation criteria, and what interviewers look for at different seniority levels.
- **Common Question Patterns:** recognizing recurring patterns and typical problems asked at FAANG companies.
- **Communication During Design:** thinking aloud effectively, explaining trade-offs, and collaborating with your interviewer.
- **Handling Ambiguity:** clarifying requirements, making assumptions, and navigating incomplete information.
- **Practice and Preparation Strategies:** building muscle memory through mock interviews and structured preparation frameworks.
