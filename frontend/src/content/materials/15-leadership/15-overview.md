# Topic 15: Technical Leadership & System Design

## Overview

This topic covers the skills needed to move beyond senior individual contributor to technical leadership: system design, architectural decision-making, technical communication, and influencing without authority. These skills are essential for staff, principal, and architect roles.

**Prerequisites:** Significant experience as a senior developer, interest in broader technical influence

**Outcome:** Lead technical initiatives, make and communicate architectural decisions, and mentor other engineers.

## Practical Exercises

1. Write an ADR for a significant technical decision in your current or past work
2. Design a system (URL shortener, rate limiter, or similar) with capacity estimates
3. Conduct a code review focused on mentoring a junior developer
4. Create a technical debt inventory and prioritization for a codebase
5. Practice system design problems with a peer or through mock interviews
