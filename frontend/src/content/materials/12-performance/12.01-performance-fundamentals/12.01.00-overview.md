# Chapter 12.1: Performance Engineering Fundamentals

## Overview

Understand performance engineering as a discipline: defining performance goals, key metrics (latency, throughput, resource utilization), and the importance of measuring before optimizing. Performance optimization without measurement is guesswork. Establishing clear goals and metrics ensures you optimize what matters and know when you've succeeded.

## Key Concepts

- **Performance requirements and SLAs:** Defining service level agreements and objectives for your application.
- **Latency vs throughput:** Understanding the difference and when each matters.
- **Percentiles (p50, p95, p99):** Why percentiles tell the truth about performance while averages lie.
- **Resource utilization metrics:** CPU, memory, and other system resources that impact performance.
- **Performance testing vs production monitoring:** Different approaches for different stages of the lifecycle.
