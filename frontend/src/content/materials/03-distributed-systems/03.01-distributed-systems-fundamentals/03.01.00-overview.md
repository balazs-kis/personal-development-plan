# Chapter 3.1: Distributed Systems Fundamentals

## Overview
This chapter covers the core challenges and fundamental concepts of distributed computing. You'll learn why distributed systems behave differently from single-node applications, understand the fundamental limitations described by the CAP theorem and the fallacies of distributed computing, and explore critical issues like network unreliability, clock synchronization, and data consistency.

## Key Concepts
- **The 8 Fallacies of Distributed Computing:** understanding the misconceptions that lead to poor design.
- **CAP Theorem:** trade-offs between Consistency, Availability, and Partition tolerance.
- **Network Partitions:** how to handle split-brain scenarios and partial failures.
- **Clock Skew and Ordering:** challenges with time and event ordering in distributed systems.
- **Exactly-Once Delivery:** why it's a myth and what to aim for instead.
