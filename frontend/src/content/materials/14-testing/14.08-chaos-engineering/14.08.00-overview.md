# Chapter 14.8: Chaos Engineering Basics

## Overview
Introduction to chaos engineering: principles, controlled experiments, fault injection, and building confidence through controlled failure.

## Key Concepts
- **Chaos Engineering Principles:** the discipline of experimenting on systems to build confidence in their resilience.
- **Hypothesis-Driven Experiments:** structured approach to testing system behavior under failure conditions.
- **Fault Injection Techniques:** methods for introducing controlled failures into systems.
- **Steady State and Blast Radius:** defining normal behavior and limiting experiment impact.
- **Production Experiments:** safely running chaos experiments in production environments.
