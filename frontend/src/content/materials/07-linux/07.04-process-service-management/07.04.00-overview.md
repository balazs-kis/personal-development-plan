# Chapter 7.4: Process and Service Management

## Overview
This chapter teaches you to understand and manage Linux processes and services using systemd. You'll learn about process states and lifecycle, systemd service units, service management with systemctl, logging with journald, and how to create your own custom services for applications.

## Key Concepts
- **Process States and Lifecycle:** understanding running, sleeping, stopped, and zombie states.
- **systemd Architecture:** learning the init system that manages services on modern Linux.
- **Service Management:** using systemctl to start, stop, enable, and manage services.
- **Journal Logging:** viewing and filtering logs with journalctl.
- **Creating Custom Services:** writing systemd unit files for your own applications.
